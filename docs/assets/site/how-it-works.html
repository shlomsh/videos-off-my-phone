How it works

Videos Off My Phone moves your biggest videos from Google Photos to your own YouTube channel, in full original quality. It checks every copy, then deletes the originals after your yes. Your phone and your Google storage get the space back.

  1. 1

    Choose. Your videos are listed biggest first. Tick the ones to move, or to delete.

  2. 2

    Move. They go straight from Google Photos to your YouTube channel. You choose who can watch: only you, people with the link, or everyone.

  3. 3

    Delete. Each copy is checked against the original. After your yes, the original goes to the Google Photos trash, where you can restore it for 30 days.

What it uses, and what it never does

Google Photos

It works in your own signed-in Google Photos tab: it lists your videos and their sizes, reads the video files to send them to YouTube, and moves an original to the trash only after you confirm it.

YouTube

With your permission it uploads videos to your channel and reads their details back, to check that each copy is complete. It never deletes anything on YouTube.

Your data stays with you

Videos go straight from Google to Google through your browser. The app keeps its notes on your computer only. There is no server of ours, no analytics and no ads.

Open source

Every line is public, so anyone can check what it does. Read the code.

Google sign-in, and Google Photos

The app asks Google for two YouTube permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the videos you choose.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm your channel, and to check each uploaded copy before anything is deleted.

Google Photos has no public interface for this, so the app uses the same internal web calls the Google Photos website uses, in your own open Google Photos tab, with your existing session there. It never sees your password. If Google changes those calls, the app stops and deletes nothing.

The app uses YouTube API Services: by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and our privacy policy. You can remove the app’s access at any time on Google’s third-party access page.

Videos Off My Phone’s use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy and the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Does YouTube really not use my Google storage?
Google’s own page on how storage works lists Gmail, Google Drive and Google Photos as sharing your storage. YouTube is not on that list. Google can change its rules, and YouTube has daily upload limits.
Is the quality the same?
The original file is uploaded, in full quality. YouTube makes its own versions to play, and it does not give back the original file, so keep anything you may need to edit later.
When does my phone get the space back?
When Google Photos on your phone removes the deleted video. The app shows the taps on an iPhone. Your phone may keep it in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
What does it cost?
Nothing. It is free and open source.
