Privacy policy

Effective [date]. Videos Off My Phone is a free, open source Chrome extension made by [developer name]. Contact: [contact email].

In short

The app runs in your browser. It has no server, no account and no analytics. Your videos go straight from Google Photos to your own YouTube channel. Nothing is sent to us, sold or shared.

What the app accesses, and why

WhatWhy
Your signed-in Google Photos page (photos.google.com), in your own browser tabTo list your videos and their sizes, to read a video file so it can be uploaded, and to move an original to the Google Photos trash after you confirm it.
YouTube, with your permission: upload videos (youtube.upload) and view your YouTube account (youtube.readonly)To upload the videos you choose to your channel, with the visibility you choose, and to read their details back so the app can check that each copy is complete.

The app does not read your photos, messages, contacts or anything else. It never deletes anything on YouTube.

What is stored, and where

Only on your computer, in Chrome’s storage for this extension:

  • For each video you moved or deleted: its Google Photos item id, its size and date, a checksum of the file, the YouTube video id, the visibility you chose, and the result of each check and delete.
  • The name of your YouTube channel.
  • The YouTube sign-in token, kept in Chrome’s memory for the session only.

Removing the extension deletes all of this. Video files are never saved to disk; they pass through your browser while uploading.

Sharing

We do not collect, sell, rent or share your data. There are no ads and no tracking. Data moves only between your browser, Google Photos and YouTube, as you direct.

Google API Services

Videos Off My Phone’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove the app’s access to your Google Account at any time on Google’s third-party access page.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
