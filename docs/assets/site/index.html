Good to know

  • Nothing is deleted without your yes. Deleted originals wait in the Google Photos trash for 30 days.
  • You choose who can watch each video: only you (private), anyone with the link (unlisted), or everyone.
  • Every copy is checked against the original: size, resolution, length and date.
  • Your phone gets the space back when Google Photos on your phone removes the deleted videos. The app shows the taps on an iPhone. Your phone may keep them in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Videos in shared albums stay in Google Photos and are never deleted, so they free no space.
  • YouTube does not give the original file back. Keep another copy of anything you can’t replace.
  • YouTube allows about 100 uploads a day for the whole app, shared by all users.
  • Google shows an “unverified app” warning at sign-in until Google’s verification finishes.

Google sign-in, and Google Photos

The app asks Google for two YouTube permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the videos you choose.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to confirm your channel, and to check each uploaded copy before anything is deleted.

Google Photos has no public interface for this, so the app uses the same internal web calls the Google Photos website uses, in your own open Google Photos tab, with your existing session there. It never sees your password. If Google changes those calls, the app stops and deletes nothing.
