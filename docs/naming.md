# Naming research: a better name than "Video Offload"

Date: 2026-11-26. Method: web search only. Nothing was registered, signed in or submitted.

## Key limits of this research
- I have no keyword-volume tool (no Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Trends).
- Popularity below is a judgement from evidence: how many guides, help pages and tools exist per phrase.
- Autocomplete lists and Reddit threads did not come back from the search tool. So no autocomplete data.
- .com and GitHub checks are search-only guesses. Check the real registrars and GitHub before use.

## 1. Search phrases people use
Judged by how many guides and official help pages target each phrase.

| Phrase | Evidence | Popularity (judgement) |
|---|---|---|
| Google Photos storage full | Many Google help pages, plus How-To Geek, Laptop Mag, Android Authority, Business Today (Aug 2026) | Very high |
| free up Google Photos storage / free up space | Same guides; Google "Clean up space" feature | Very high |
| Google storage full | Shared quota across Drive, Gmail, Photos; many guides | Very high |
| delete videos from Google Photos | Many how-tos and 5+ bulk-delete extensions | High |
| large videos Google Photos / find large videos | Google "Large photos and videos" tool; guides say videos are most of the usage | High |
| iPhone storage full videos | Many iOS cleaner apps (VidClean, Video Remover, Large Video Cleaner, Delete Large Videos Fast) | Very high, but crowded with iPhone cleaners |
| delete from phone after backup | Google help page "Free up space on your device"; Apple thread on clearing iPhone after Google Photos | High |
| upload Google Photos video to YouTube | A few guides (MiniTool etc.). All say there is no direct way. Download, then upload | Low to medium. Few searchers, little competition |
| back up videos to YouTube / YouTube as free video storage | Blog posts only. Mentions Public, Private, Unlisted | Low |
| Google Photos alternative / unlimited video storage | Many roundups (Amazon Photos, IDrive, Ente, Flickr) | High, but a different intent (switching service) |

What this means:
- The big, popular phrases are about the problem: "storage full", "free up", "delete videos".
- "YouTube" is the how, not the search term. Few people search for it. Use it in the description, not the name.
- The gap: nobody found offers "move big videos to YouTube, verify, then delete". Competing tools only delete or only compress.

## 2. Naming rules (official)
Google Photos and Chrome Web Store:
- Do not use Google trademarks, or confusingly similar marks, as your extension or company name without written permission.
- You may say you work with a Google product using "for", "for use with" or "compatible with", with the trademark symbol. Example given: "for Google Chrome(TM)".
- When a Google trademark is in the title or description, add attribution: "[Product] is a trademark of Google LLC."
- Do not use a Google trademark as your logo.
- Google brand terms: no registering domains or brand names confusingly similar to Google's.
  The terms page links to separate trademark guidelines but does not spell out the "for" phrasing itself.
- Judgement: "Google Photos" in the store title as "... for Google Photos" is tolerated by the written rule, but it is the riskiest place. The safest place is the short description and long description.

YouTube (stricter):
- YouTube API branding guidelines: never use "YouTube", "YT", "You-Tube" or any variant in the name of your app.
- Do not register YouTube marks as second-level domains, or build product, service, feature or company names from them.
- You may say the app is "for YouTube" or works with YouTube, for example in the description.
- These guidelines are part of the YouTube API Terms of Service. This app uses the YouTube API, so they bind it.
- Breach means a request to change the app, or to stop using the marks.

Chrome Web Store spam and impersonation:
- Keyword spam: no misleading, irrelevant or excessive metadata in title, description, developer name, icon or screenshots.
  Lists of brands or keywords without added value are banned. Repeating one keyword more than 5 times draws extra scrutiny.
- Impersonation: do not claim the product is authorised, endorsed or made by another company. The test is whether a user could think it is Google's.
- Do not look like part of the browser, the OS or the store.
- Possible removal or reduced visibility if IP infringement is suspected.

Practical rule: the name has no "Google", no "Photos" brand use, no "YouTube". The keywords live in the store title suffix, short description and long description, with attribution.

## 3. Competitors and clashes
Chrome Web Store (all are delete tools, none move originals):
- Delete All Google Photos (most reviews, freemium; also on Firefox)
- Google Photos Cleaner (filters videos; paywalled features)
- Bulk Delete Google Photos
- Google Photos Bulk Delete Chrome Extension
- Google Photos Delete Tool
- Easy Organizer and Backup for Google Photos (local album backup)
- PicSweep (Google Photos and iCloud cleaner)
- Uploader Extension (batch upload local files to YouTube)

Other:
- iPhone cleaners: VidClean, Video Remover, Large Video Cleaner, Delete Large Videos Fast, Video Compress, GOM Saver, Spaceful (has a "Video Space Saver" feature), Space Saver-Phone Cleanup.
- Offload apps: DataOffloader, OffloadPro (USB drive), Red Giant Offload (camera footage), OffloadBuddy (GitHub, camera footage).
- "Video Offload for VideoPress": a WordPress plugin. Exact-ish name clash with the current name.
- Google Takeout and the Photos "Clean up space" tool are the built-in options.

Finding: the current name "Video Offload" has no exact app clash, but it is weak. "Offload" is a media-pro word, not what families type. The WordPress plugin owns the phrase.

## 4. Candidates
TM risk is about the name itself. "Clash" is what I found searching. Domains were not checked at a registrar.

1. **Big Video Mover**
   - Repo: `big-video-mover`
   - Store title: "Big Video Mover - Free Up Storage for Google Photos(TM)"
   - Short desc: "Move big videos from Google Photos to your YouTube channel in full quality, check they match, then free up storage."
   - Matches: large videos, free up storage, move videos Google Photos to YouTube.
   - TM risk: low (name). Low-medium (title suffix).
   - Clash: no exact match found. Names like Big Video (Firefox, different), MOVER Recorder (different).
   - Domain/GitHub: not verified. Likely contested for .com; try bigvideomover.com or an app subdomain.

2. **Free Up Storage for Google Photos**
   - Repo: `free-up-photos-storage`
   - Store title: same as name. Short desc: "Free up Google Photos storage by moving big videos to YouTube and deleting the originals."
   - Matches: the top three phrases exactly.
   - TM risk: medium. The "for" form is allowed in writing, but Google may still object.
   - Clash: no exact match, but a very crowded space of generic names.
   - Domain: generic, almost certainly taken.

3. **Videos Off My Phone**
   - Repo: `videos-off-my-phone`
   - Store title: "Videos Off My Phone - Free Up Storage for Google Photos(TM)"
   - Matches: iPhone storage full videos, free up phone storage.
   - TM risk: low.
   - Clash: none found for the exact phrase.
   - Domain: unknown; the exact phrase looks plausible to be free. Verify.

4. **Video Space Saver**
   - Repo: `video-space-saver`
   - Matches: iPhone storage full videos, save space.
   - TM risk: low.
   - Clash: medium. Spaceful uses it as a feature name; many "Space Saver" iPhone apps.

5. **Big Video Cleaner**
   - Repo: `big-video-cleaner`
   - Matches: delete videos, large videos.
   - TM risk: low.
   - Clash: medium. Close to "Large Video Cleaner" (iOS), "VidClean". It also says "cleaner", but this app keeps a copy, so it undersells the YouTube step.

6. **Storage Freer**
   - Repo: `storage-freer`
   - Matches: free up storage.
   - TM risk: low. Clash: none found, but an unusual word and weak search match.

7. **Video Offload** (current)
   - Repo: `video-offload`
   - TM risk: low.
   - Clash: medium. WordPress plugin "Video Offload for VideoPress". No hit for a GitHub repo of that name in search (not conclusive).
   - Weak: "offload" is not a word families type.

8. **Move Videos to YouTube**
   - Rejected. Contains "YouTube" in the name. This breaks the YouTube API branding rule. High risk.

9. **Google Photos Video Mover**
   - Rejected. Puts "Google Photos" in the name itself. High risk, impersonation look.

10. **Clear Phone Videos**
    - Repo: `clear-phone-videos`
    - Matches: iPhone storage full videos.
    - TM risk: low. Clash: none found; a crowded cleaner space and no sign of the move step.

## 5. Top 3 ranked
1. **Big Video Mover** - plain words, says "move" (the real difference from delete-only tools), has no Google or YouTube marks, and the store title suffix carries "Free Up Storage for Google Photos".
2. **Videos Off My Phone** - matches how an ordinary person describes the pain, no mark risk, no exact clash found. Slightly long.
3. **Free Up Storage for Google Photos** - best match to the top searches, but "Google Photos" sits in the name, so keep it as the store title suffix and not the app name.

Main constraint: never put "YouTube" (or YT) in the app name or domain. Keep "Google Photos" out of the name too, and use it only after "for" in the title and descriptions, with the attribution "Google Photos and YouTube are trademarks of Google LLC."

Next steps (not done): check the .com and GitHub name at a registrar and on GitHub; ask the Chrome Web Store dashboard for a draft check before publishing; consider the Google/YouTube brand request forms if you want the suffix in the title.
